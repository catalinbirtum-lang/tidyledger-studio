How it works

Simple, fixed-price and done mostly by message – no meetings needed.

  1. Tell me about your business. A short questionnaire: services, prices, hours, area, photos and your top customer questions.
  2. I build your site and chatbot. You get a private preview link within the delivery time.
  3. You review. Ask for changes – revisions are included in every package.
  4. Go live. I publish it on free hosting in your own account (or hand over the files) and connect your domain if you have one.

What the chatbot is (and isn't)

It's a fast, private FAQ assistant that runs in the visitor's browser and answers from the information you give me – hours, prices, services, policies – and collects booking or quote requests. It has no monthly fees and never makes things up, because it only says what you've approved. It is not a ChatGPT-style AI that improvises; if you want one of those connected to your own AI account, I can quote for it separately.

Questions

Or ask the studio assistant (bottom right) – it's the same chatbot your customers would use.

How long does it take?

Starter: 4 days, Business: 7 days, Premium: 10 days – counted from when I have your info and photos. Express delivery is available on Starter and Business for £40.

Is hosting really free?

Yes. Your site is a fast static site, so it can live on free hosting such as Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or GitHub Pages, in an account you own. The only cost you might have is your domain name (typically around £10–£15 a year for a .co.uk, paid to a registrar of your choice).

What do you need from me?

Your business name and logo (if you have one), services and prices, opening hours, the area you cover, 5–15 photos (or I use simple graphics), your top customer questions, and where booking requests should go (an email address). I send a short questionnaire.

Is the chatbot real AI?

It's a rule-based FAQ assistant: it matches visitors' questions (even with typos) to answers you've approved, so it never invents prices or promises, and it costs nothing to run. I use AI tools to build your site faster, but the chatbot itself doesn't need an AI subscription. A ChatGPT-style bot on your own AI account can be quoted separately.

Do I own the website?

Yes – you get all the files and the hosting account is yours. No lock-in: you can edit it yourself or take it to anyone else.

Can you connect my domain?

Yes, on Business and Premium (or as a small extra on Starter). You keep the domain in your own registrar account; I talk you through the DNS settings or do it with you.

Can it take bookings?

The chatbot collects booking or quote requests and sends them to your email. On Premium I can also link your free booking tool (e.g. Google Calendar appointment page, Calendly free plan, or the booking page you already use).

Do I need a care plan?

No. The site keeps running on free hosting without one. The care plan is just for people who'd rather send a message than edit things themselves.

Do you have reviews or past clients?

Honest answer: the studio is new, so there are no client reviews yet – the demos on this page are invented sample businesses. That's why the first projects are priced low, with revisions included.

How do I order or contact you?

Please order or message through Fiverr or Upwork (search for Tidy Ledger UK) – you get buyer protection and simple payment.

What's not included?

Online shops/payments, custom logo design, paid ads, ongoing SEO campaigns, WordPress plugins, and anything needing paid software are outside these packages – ask and I'll tell you honestly if I can help.
