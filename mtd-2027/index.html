Does Making Tax Digital apply to me from April 2027?

A plain-English checklist for UK sole traders and landlords. Every rule below comes from GOV.UK and links to the page it came from. Facts checked 14 October 2026; GOV.UK always wins if it changes.

Official information (GOV.UK)

1. The start dates

Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax applies if you are a sole trader or landlord registered for Self Assessment, you get self-employment and/or property income, and your qualifying income is more than the threshold:

Qualifying incomeIn tax yearMust use MTD from
over £50,0002024 to 20256 April 2026 (already started)
over £30,0002025 to 20266 April 2027
over £20,0002026 to 20276 April 2028

HMRC checks your Self Assessment return and writes to you if you are over the threshold. If no letter arrives, it is still your responsibility to check. Partnerships do not need to use it yet.

Source: GOV.UK: Find out if and when you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

2. Checklist: is my qualifying income over £30,000?

  • I add up my self-employment turnover (income before expenses), not my profit.
  • I add my gross property income (UK, and foreign property if I was UK resident). For a jointly owned property, I count only my share.
  • I do not count wages (PAYE), dividends, State or private pensions, or my share of profit from a partnership.
  • If I traded for less than 12 months as a sole trader, HMRC annualises the figure (e.g. 6 months is doubled).
  • Example from GOV.UK: £25,000 rent + £27,000 self-employment = £52,000 qualifying income.

Source: GOV.UK: Work out your qualifying income for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

3. Checklist: could I be exempt?

  • Qualifying income of £20,000 or less: automatically exempt.
  • No National Insurance number before the start of the tax year: automatically exempt.
  • Some automatic exemptions come from what was on my 2024 to 2025 return (for example: Minister of religion SA102M, Lloyd's member SA103L, Married Couple's or Blind Person's Allowance; and, until April 2027 only, averaging relief, qualifying care relief, SA107 or SA109 pages).
  • Digitally excluded (age, health, disability, religious belief, or no possible internet access): I can apply to HMRC. Being unfamiliar with software, or extra cost, is not accepted as a reason on its own.
  • If I'm exempt I still file a normal Self Assessment return.

Source: GOV.UK: Find out if you can get an exemption from Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

4. What I'll have to do once I'm in

  • Keep digital records of self-employment and property income and expenses.
  • Send a quarterly update for each business. Deadlines: 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May. Updates are summaries, not tax returns; send one even if there was no income.
  • Submit my tax return through software by 31 January the following year.
  • Late quarterly updates earn penalty points; 4 points means a £200 penalty. HMRC is not applying points for late quarterly updates in the 2026 to 2027 tax year only; from 2027 to 2028 they apply.

Source: GOV.UK: Send quarterly updates (Use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax)

5. Software

  • I need HMRC-recognised compatible software: either software that creates the records, or bridging software that connects to my spreadsheet.
  • Free products exist for simple affairs, often with limits. HMRC does not recommend any product. Use HMRC's software finder tool.
  • Choose software before signing up.

Source: GOV.UK: Choose the right software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax
