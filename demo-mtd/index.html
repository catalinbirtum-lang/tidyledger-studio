How it works

YOU DO (your own logins)
  1. Create or find your Government Gateway login
  2. Sign up for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax on GOV.UK
  3. Connect your software to HMRC with your own login
  4. Send quarterly updates and submit your tax return using your software
WE HELP
  1. Choose MTD-compatible software that fits you
  2. Set-up walkthrough: categories, receipts, settings
  3. Guidance to link your bank feed
  4. 1-to-1 training so you're confident
  5. Deadline reminders every quarter

We never ask for your passwords or tax reference, and we do not access or prepare your records.
